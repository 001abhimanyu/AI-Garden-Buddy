OPEN INNOVATION

AI that belongs to you

AI Garden Buddy is designed around open AI principles.

🔓

Open Models

Built around open-weight AI models rather than depending entirely on a closed AI API.

💻

Browser First

The project is designed to work directly in the browser using HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

🔐

Privacy

Garden information can stay on the user's device instead of being sent to a traditional AI service.

🌍

Real World Impact

The goal isn't more screen time. The goal is helping people spend more time outdoors.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

AI should help us experience more of the real world.

AI Garden Buddy was created for the Hacktoberfest Open-Source AI Challenge: Week 1 — Touch Grass.

Instead of using AI to keep people staring at a screen, this project uses AI to create a simple plan and then encourages the user to step outside.
